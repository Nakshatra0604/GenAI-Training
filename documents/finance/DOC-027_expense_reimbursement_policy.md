# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may submit expenses for business-related costs such as client
meals, approved travel, and necessary equipment not provided by the
company. Expenses are submitted through the expense portal with a receipt
attached for any amount over twenty-five dollars.

Expense reports should be submitted within thirty days of the expense being
incurred. Reports submitted after ninety days require manager approval and
a brief explanation for the delay, and may be denied at the manager's
discretion if the delay is excessive.

Meal expenses have a daily cap that varies by city cost of living, listed
in the expense portal's reference table. Alcohol is reimbursable only in
the context of a client meal and only up to a modest portion of the total
bill. Personal expenses incidentally mixed into a business trip, such as a
personal side trip, are not reimbursable and should be excluded from the
report.

Managers approve expense reports within five business days. Reimbursement
is processed in the next payroll cycle following approval.
