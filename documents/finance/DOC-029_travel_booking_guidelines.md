# Travel Booking Guidelines

Business travel should be booked through the approved travel booking tool
whenever possible, since it consolidates billing and provides duty-of-care
tracking in case of an emergency during travel. Flights should generally be
booked economy class for trips under six hours and may be booked premium
economy for longer international flights, subject to manager approval.

Hotel bookings should stay within the nightly rate guideline for the
destination city listed in the travel tool, with exceptions requiring
manager approval when the guideline rate is not reasonably available.
Ground transportation should favor the most practical reasonable option;
ride-share and taxis are acceptable for most trips, with a rental car
reserved for trips where public transport or ride-share is impractical.

Travel booked outside the approved tool, such as directly through an
airline for a schedule reason, is still reimbursable but should be flagged
in the expense report, since it will not appear automatically in the
travel system's records.
