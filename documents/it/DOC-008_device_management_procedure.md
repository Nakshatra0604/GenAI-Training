# Device Management Procedure

## Device Enrollment

All company-owned laptops and phones must be enrolled in the mobile device
management (MDM) system before being used to access company email or
internal tools. Enrollment is typically completed by IT during initial
setup, either before a new hire's first day or during a scheduled
appointment.

## Standard Configuration

Enrolled devices receive disk encryption, a managed firewall
configuration, and automatic security updates. Employees should not disable
these controls; doing so triggers an automated compliance alert to IT
Security.

## Personal Devices

Personal phones may be used to access company email and calendar through
the approved mobile app, which enforces a passcode and remote-wipe
capability for the company data container only. Personal devices are not
otherwise managed by IT and are not required to install the full MDM
profile.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

A lost or stolen device should be reported to the IT Helpdesk immediately.
IT can remotely lock or wipe an enrolled device. Employees should also
change passwords for any accounts that were logged in on the device as a
precaution.

## Device Retirement

When an employee leaves the company or receives a replacement device, the
old device is returned to IT for a full wipe and reimaging before it is
reassigned or decommissioned. Devices are not reassigned without going
through this process.
