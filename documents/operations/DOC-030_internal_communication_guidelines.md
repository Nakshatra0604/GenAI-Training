# Internal Communication Guidelines

NexaCore Solutions uses chat for quick, informal communication and email
for anything that needs a clear record or involves people outside the
immediate team. Chat channels are organized by team and topic, and
company-wide announcements are reserved for information genuinely relevant
to everyone, not general team updates.

Response time expectations differ by channel: chat messages are generally
expected to be answered within a business day, while email may reasonably
take longer, particularly for non-urgent requests. Urgent, time-sensitive
issues should use a direct message or phone call rather than a channel
post, since channel posts can be easily missed.

Meetings should have a clear purpose and, where practical, an agenda shared
in advance. Meetings that are primarily informational, without a need for
discussion, are often better replaced with a written update, since this
respects people's time and creates a searchable record.
