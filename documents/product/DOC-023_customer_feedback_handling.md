# Customer Feedback Handling

Customer feedback arrives through several channels: in-app feedback forms,
support tickets, sales conversations, and direct outreach from the product
team. All feedback is logged in the shared feedback tracker regardless of
source, so patterns can be identified across channels rather than staying
siloed within one team.

## Triage

Product managers triage new feedback weekly, tagging it by product area
and rough theme. Feedback that reveals a bug rather than a feature request
is redirected to engineering through the normal bug reporting process
instead of being tracked as a feature request.

## Prioritization

Feedback volume alone does not determine priority; a request raised by
many customers with a low individual impact may be weighed differently
than a request from a small number of customers representing significant
revenue. Product managers make this judgment call during quarterly
planning, with input from sales and customer success.

## Closing the Loop

When feedback leads to a shipped feature, customers who originally
requested it are notified where practically possible, since this is one of
the strongest signals customers get that their input is taken seriously.
Feedback that will not be actioned is also closed out with a brief
explanation rather than left open indefinitely.
