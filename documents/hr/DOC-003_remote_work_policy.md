# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility   

Most roles at NexaCore Solutions are eligible for a hybrid arrangement of
three office days and two remote days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require manager and department head approval and are evaluated on a
role-by-role basis, typically for positions with limited in-person
collaboration needs.

## Expectations While Working Remotely

Employees working remotely are expected to be reachable during core hours
(10:00 to 16:00 local time) and to attend scheduled meetings with video on
unless otherwise agreed with the team. Remote work does not change
deliverable expectations; performance is measured on output, not physical
presence.

## Equipment and Expenses

NexaCore Solutions provides a laptop and a one-time home office stipend for
employees approved for regular remote work. Ongoing costs such as internet
and electricity are the employee's responsibility unless the role is
classified as fully remote, in which case a monthly allowance applies.

## Security Requirements While Remote

Employees must connect to internal systems through the company VPN and
must not use public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks to access confidential data.
Screens should be locked when unattended, and physical documents containing
sensitive information should not be left visible in shared or public
spaces.

## Requesting a Temporary Remote Arrangement

Temporary remote work (for example, during travel or a short-term personal
circumstance) can be requested directly with a manager without a formal
policy exception, provided it does not exceed two consecutive weeks.
