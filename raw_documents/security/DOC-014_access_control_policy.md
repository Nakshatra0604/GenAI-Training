# Access Control Policy

Access to NexaCore Solutions systems is granted based on role and business
need, following the principle of least privilege described in the
Information Security Policy. New access is requested through the access
request portal and requires approval from the employee's manager and the
relevant system owner.

## Standard Access

Standard access, such as email, calendar, and general company tools, is
provisioned automatically as part of onboarding based on the employee's
department. No separate request is needed for these baseline systems.

## Elevated Access

Elevated access, such as production system access or financial system
access, requires a documented business justification and is time-limited
where practical. Elevated access is reviewed every quarter, and access that
is no longer needed is removed rather than left in place by default.

## Access Reviews

System owners conduct a quarterly review of everyone with access to their
system, confirming that each person's access still matches their current
role. Access reviews are a compliance requirement and are tracked
centrally by IT Security, with overdue reviews escalated to department
heads.

## Offboarding

When an employee leaves NexaCore Solutions, all system access is revoked
on their last working day, coordinated between People Operations and IT.
For roles with access to highly sensitive systems, access may be revoked
earlier if the departure is involuntary, in coordination with the
employee's manager.

## Shared and Service Accounts

Shared accounts are discouraged and require explicit approval from IT
Security. Service accounts used by automated systems must have their
credentials stored in the approved secrets management tool and must not be
used interactively by individual employees.
