# Procurement Procedure

## Purchase Requests

Purchases over five hundred dollars require a purchase request submitted
through the procurement portal, including the business justification and
the budget code the purchase will be charged against. Purchases under this
threshold can generally be made directly by the employee and expensed
through the standard expense process.

## Approval Thresholds

Purchase requests up to five thousand dollars require manager approval.
Requests above five thousand dollars require both manager and department
head approval, and requests above twenty-five thousand dollars additionally
require finance team review before a purchase order is issued.

## Preferred Vendors

For common categories such as software and office equipment, a list of
preferred vendors with pre-negotiated pricing is maintained by the
procurement team. Using a preferred vendor is not mandatory but is
encouraged, since it typically speeds up approval and often results in
better pricing.

## Receiving and Payment

Once goods or services are received, the requester confirms receipt in the
procurement portal, which triggers the payment process. Payment terms with
vendors are typically net thirty days from invoice unless a different term
is negotiated as part of the contract.
