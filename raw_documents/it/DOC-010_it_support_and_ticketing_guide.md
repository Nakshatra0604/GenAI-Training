# IT Support and Ticketing Guide

## How to Reach IT Support

The IT Helpdesk can be reached through the internal ticketing portal, the
#it-help chat channel, or by phone for urgent issues affecting an entire
team or floor. Non-urgent requests should always go through the ticketing
portal so they can be tracked and prioritized correctly.

## Ticket Priorities

- Critical: an outage affecting many people or a security incident
- High: a single employee is fully blocked from working
- Medium: a feature or tool is broken but a workaround exists
- Low: general requests, such as software installs or account changes

## Response Time Targets

Critical tickets are acknowledged within thirty minutes during business
hours. High-priority tickets are acknowledged within two hours. Medium and
low priority tickets are typically addressed within one to three business
days depending on current ticket volume.

## What to Include in a Ticket

A good ticket includes what was expected to happen, what actually
happened, any error message shown, and steps already tried. Screenshots
significantly speed up resolution for visual issues such as display
problems or unexpected application behavior.

## Escalation

If a ticket has not received a response within its target time, it can be
escalated by replying to the ticket or messaging the IT team lead directly.
Escalation is not needed for tickets that are already in progress with
regular updates.
