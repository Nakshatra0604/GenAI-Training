# Vendor Management Procedure

## Vendor Onboarding

New vendors that will access company systems or handle company or customer
data go through a review that includes a security questionnaire and, for
higher-risk vendors, a review of their own security certifications. Vendors
that will not access systems or data, such as an office supplies vendor,
follow a lighter procurement-only process.

## Contracts

All vendor contracts are reviewed by Legal before signing, and contracts
involving data processing include a data processing agreement consistent
with the company's data handling requirements. Contract renewals are
tracked centrally so that expiring contracts are reviewed before they lapse
rather than renewing automatically without review.

## Ongoing Vendor Review

Vendors with access to Restricted data are reviewed annually, covering any
changes to their security posture, incident history, and continued
business need for the relationship. Vendor access is included in the
quarterly access review alongside employee access.

## Vendor Offboarding

When a vendor relationship ends, all access granted to that vendor is
revoked, and any company data held by the vendor is confirmed deleted or
returned according to the terms of the contract. The vendor is removed from
the active vendor register once offboarding is confirmed complete.
