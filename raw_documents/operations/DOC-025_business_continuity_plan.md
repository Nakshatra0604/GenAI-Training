# Business Continuity Plan

## 1. Purpose

This plan describes how NexaCore Solutions continues critical operations
during a significant disruption, such as an extended office closure,
regional infrastructure outage, or loss of a key facility.

## 2. Critical Functions

Critical functions are those that, if interrupted for more than a defined
tolerance period, would cause significant harm to customers or the
business. These include core product infrastructure, customer support,
and payroll processing. Each critical function has an assigned owner
responsible for maintaining its continuity plan.

## 3. Recovery Time Objectives

Each critical function has a recovery time objective describing how
quickly it must be restored. Core product infrastructure has a recovery
time objective of four hours; customer support has a recovery time
objective of one business day; payroll processing has a recovery time
objective of three business days.

## 4. Office Disruption

If an office location becomes unavailable, employees are expected to work
remotely using existing remote work capabilities rather than a
special-purpose continuity process, since most roles already have the
tools needed to work from home.

## 5. Infrastructure Failure

Core infrastructure is deployed across multiple independent regions so that
a failure in one region does not require manual failover for most
services. Services without automatic multi-region failover are documented
in the infrastructure continuity register along with their manual failover
procedure.

## 6. Communication During a Disruption

A designated communications lead is responsible for keeping employees
informed during a significant disruption, using a channel that does not
depend on the affected infrastructure, such as a separate status
communication tool. Customers are informed through the status page and, for
extended disruptions, direct communication from customer success.

## 7. Plan Testing

Critical function owners test their continuity procedures at least
annually, either through a tabletop exercise or, where practical, a live
failover test. Test results and any gaps identified are documented and
tracked to resolution.

## 8. Plan Maintenance

This plan is reviewed annually and after any significant organizational
change, such as a new critical system or a change in office locations.
