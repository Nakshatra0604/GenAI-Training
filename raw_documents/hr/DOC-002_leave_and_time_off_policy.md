# Leave and Time Off Policy

NexaCore Solutions provides employees with paid time off, sick leave, and
public holidays. Full-time employees accrue fifteen days of paid time off
per year during their first three years, increasing to twenty days after
three years of continuous employment.

Leave requests should be submitted through the internal HR portal at least
five business days in advance for planned absences. Sick leave does not
require advance notice, but employees should notify their manager as early
as reasonably possible on the day of absence.

Unused paid time off may be carried over up to a maximum of five days into
the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used within the
first quarter or they are forfeited. Employees on approved parental leave
continue to accrue time off during the leave period in accordance with
local employment regulations.

Managers approve or deny leave requests within two business days. Denials
must include a reason, typically related to team coverage during a
critical project period, and should offer an alternative date where
possible.
