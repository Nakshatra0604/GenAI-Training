## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct describes the standards of behavior expected from
everyone working at or on behalf of NexaCore Solutions, including
employees, contractors, and interns. It exists to protect a workplace where
people can do their best work without fear of harassment, discrimination,
or retaliation.

## 2. Respect in the Workplace

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with
professionalism and respect regardless of role, seniority, background, or
personal characteristics. Disagreement on ideas and approach is normal and
encouraged; personal attacks, belittling comments, and exclusionary
behavior are not tolerated.

## 3. Harassment and Discrimination

NexaCore Solutions prohibits harassment and discrimination in any form,
including behavior based on a protected characteristic under applicable
law. This includes unwelcome comments, jokes, physical conduct, and
retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## 4. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose situations where personal relationships or outside
financial interests could reasonably be seen to influence their business
decisions. Common examples include hiring or managing a close relative,
holding a significant financial stake in a vendor, or accepting gifts of
material value from a supplier.

### 4.1 Disclosure Process

Disclosures are made to the employee's manager and to the People Operations
team through the internal conflict-of-interest form. Most disclosures do
not require any change in duties; the purpose is transparency, not
prohibition.

## 5. Confidential Information

Employees regularly have access to information that is not public,
including product plans, financial figures, and client details. This
information should only be shared with people who have a legitimate
business need to know, and should never be discussed in public spaces or
on personal social media.

## 6. Use of Company Resources

Company equipment, accounts, and systems are provided for business
purposes. Limited personal use is acceptable as long as it does not
interfere with work, violate other policies, or create legal or security
risk for the company.

## 7. Reporting Concerns

| Concern type | Where to report | Expected response time |
|---|---|---|
| Harassment or discrimination | People Operations or Ethics Hotline | 2 business days |
| Conflict of interest | Manager and People Operations | 5 business days |
| Policy violation | Manager or Ethics Hotline | 5 business days |
| Safety concern | Facilities or Ethics Hotline | Same day |

Reports can be made anonymously through the Ethics Hotline. Retaliation
against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is itself a violation of
this Code and is subject to disciplinary action.

## 8. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this Code of Conduct are addressed on a case-by-case basis
and may range from coaching and a documented warning to termination of
employment, depending on severity, intent, and history. Serious violations,
such as harassment or falsification of records, may result in immediate
termination.

## 9. Annual Acknowledgment

All employees are required to review and acknowledge this Code of Conduct
annually through the HR portal. New hires acknowledge it as part of the
onboarding process during their first week.
