## Welcome to NexaCore Solutions

This guide walks new hires through their first two weeks at NexaCore Solutions.
It covers account setup, required training, and who to contact for common
questions during the first month.

## Before Your First Day

IT provisions a laptop, email account, and access badge roughly three
business days before your start date. New hires receive a welcome email
from the People Operations team containing a checklist and a link to the
benefits enrollment portal. Enrollment must be completed within the first
thirty days of employment.

## Week One

The first day begins with a welcome session led by People Operations,
followed by a laptop setup session with IT. Managers are expected to
schedule a one-on-one within the first three days to review team goals and
introduce the new hire to immediate teammates. Mandatory training modules
(security awareness, code of conduct, and workplace safety) must be
completed by the end of week one.

## Week Two Onward

New hires are assigned a peer buddy for the first thirty days. The buddy is
not a manager and is meant to answer day-to-day questions such as tool
access, meeting norms, and internal terminology. A thirty-day check-in with
the manager is scheduled to review early progress and adjust onboarding
tasks if needed.

## Key Contacts

- People Operations: onboarding questions, benefits, payroll setup
- IT Helpdesk: equipment, account access, software requests
- Facilities: badge access, desk assignment, parking

## Common Issues

New hires occasionally report delayed badge access on their first day. If
this happens, Facilities can issue a temporary visitor badge while the
permanent badge is processed. Laptop delivery delays should be reported to
the IT Helpdesk, which can issue a loaner device.
