# Performance Review Process

NexaCore Solutions runs two formal performance review cycles per year, in
June and December. Each cycle includes a self-assessment, manager
assessment, and a calibration step where managers within a department
compare ratings to ensure consistency across teams.

## Self-Assessment

Employees complete a self-assessment covering key accomplishments,
challenges, and goals for the next cycle. This is submitted through the
performance portal roughly two weeks before the review meeting and forms
the starting point for the manager's assessment.

## Manager Assessment

Managers review the self-assessment alongside peer feedback collected
through short surveys sent to two to four colleagues chosen by the
employee and manager together. The manager assessment focuses on impact,
collaboration, and growth against the goals set in the previous cycle.

## Calibration

Before ratings are finalized, managers within the same department meet to
review ratings collectively. This step exists to reduce inconsistency
between managers who may rate similar performance differently, and can
result in a rating being adjusted before it is shared with the employee.

## Ratings and Outcomes

Ratings fall into four categories: exceeds expectations, meets
expectations, partially meets expectations, and does not meet
expectations. Ratings feed into compensation review decisions but are
communicated to the employee separately from any compensation change,
which typically follows two to three weeks later.

## Mid-Cycle Check-ins

Managers are expected to hold informal check-ins at least once a month so
that formal review conversations do not contain unexpected feedback. Any
performance concern serious enough to affect a rating should already have
been discussed with the employee before the formal review.
