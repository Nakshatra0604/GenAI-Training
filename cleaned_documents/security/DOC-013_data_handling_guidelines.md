## Purpose

These guidelines describe how employees at NexaCore Solutions should store,
share, and dispose of company and customer data according to its
sensitivity, in line with the Information Security Policy's classification
levels.

## Storing Data

Restricted data, such as customer records or financial information, must
be stored only in approved systems that support encryption at rest and
access logging. Storing Restricted data in personal cloud storage,
personal email, or unmanaged spreadsheets on a local desktop is not
permitted under any circumstance.

## Sharing Data Internally

Internal and Restricted data should be shared only with colleagues who
have a legitimate business need, using approved internal tools. Broad
distribution lists should be used carefully; a message intended for one
team should not be sent to a company-wide list simply because it is
convenient.

## Sharing Data Externally

Sharing Restricted data with an external party, including a customer
requesting their own records, requires verification of identity and, in
most cases, approval from a manager or the Legal team. Public and most
Internal data can generally be shared externally without additional
approval, but the classification should be confirmed if there is any
doubt.

## Data Retention and Disposal

Data should not be kept longer than necessary for its business purpose.
Customer data is retained according to the schedule defined in the
company's data retention policy, and is deleted or anonymized once that
period ends. Paper documents containing Restricted data must be shredded
rather than placed in regular waste bins.

## Reporting a Data Handling Mistake

If Restricted data is sent to the wrong person or stored somewhere it
should not be, this should be reported to IT Security immediately, even if
the mistake seems minor. Early reporting allows the exposure to be
contained quickly, such as revoking a shared link before it is accessed.
