## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the baseline security principles used to protect
NexaCore Solutions information, systems, accounts, and services. It exists
so that employees have a shared, written understanding of what is expected
of them, rather than relying on informal habits that vary between teams.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and any third party
granted access to NexaCore Solutions systems or data, regardless of
whether that access is temporary or long-term. It covers company-owned
devices as well as personal devices used to access company email or
internal tools.

## 3. Data Classification

Information at NexaCore Solutions is classified into three levels: Public,
Internal, and Restricted. Public information can be shared outside the
company without approval. Internal information is intended for employees
and approved contractors only. Restricted information includes customer
data, financial records, and security configurations, and requires
explicit authorization to access or share.

## 4. Account Security

Every account must be protected by a unique password and multi-factor
authentication. Shared accounts are not permitted except for a small,
documented list of service accounts managed directly by IT Security.
Access to any system should be reviewed at least once per quarter by the
system owner.

## 5. Least Privilege

Employees are granted the minimum level of access required to perform
their role. Additional access should be requested through the access
request process rather than assumed, and temporary access granted for a
specific project should be removed once the project concludes.

## 6. Physical Security

Office badges must not be shared or lent to another person, including
colleagues who have forgotten their own badge. Visitors must be signed in
at reception and escorted at all times within restricted areas such as the
server room.

## 7. Third-Party and Vendor Access

Vendors requiring access to company systems must sign a data processing
agreement before access is granted. Vendor access is reviewed alongside
employee access during the quarterly access review and is removed promptly
when a vendor engagement ends.

## 8. Security Awareness

All employees complete mandatory security awareness training during
onboarding and annually thereafter. Training covers phishing recognition,
password hygiene, and how to report a suspected incident.

## 9. Policy Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from IT Security and
must include a business justification, a description of the compensating
control, and a review date. Exceptions are tracked in a central register
and revisited at each review date rather than left open indefinitely.

## 10. Review

This policy is reviewed annually by IT Security and after any significant
security incident. Employees are notified of material changes through the
internal announcements channel.
