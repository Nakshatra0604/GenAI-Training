# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy governs the use of NexaCore Solutions computing resources,
including laptops, email, internal systems, and network access. It applies
to all employees, contractors, and any third party granted access to
company systems.

## Permitted Use

Company systems are provided primarily for business purposes. Reasonable
personal use is permitted, such as checking personal email or browsing
during a break, provided it does not consume excessive bandwidth, install
unapproved software, or expose the company to legal or security risk.

## Prohibited Activities

Employees must not use company systems to access illegal content, install
unlicensed software, attempt to bypass security controls, or share company
credentials with anyone else, including colleagues covering for them during
absence. Running personal business ventures on company equipment is also
prohibited.

## Software Installation

Only software approved through the IT software catalog may be installed on
company devices without a request. Installation of unapproved software,
including browser extensions with broad data access, requires a request
through the IT ticketing system and a brief security review.

## Monitoring

NexaCore Solutions reserves the right to monitor use of company systems for
security and compliance purposes, in line with applicable local law.
Monitoring is not used to track personal communications unrelated to
security or policy violations.

## Enforcement

Violations of this policy are handled by IT Security in coordination with
People Operations and may result in access suspension while an
investigation is conducted.
